# Y-Gaming Personal Hub — Product Brief

**Version:** 0.2  
**Domain:** y-gaming.in  
**Platform:** GitHub Pages  
**Status:** Product definition

---

# 1. Vision

Create a personal digital hub that represents **Simon** as a person rather than functioning solely as a portfolio.

The website brings together Simon's:

- Professional career
- Independent projects
- Creative work
- Gaming and creator activities
- Hobbies and interests
- Current experiments and things he's learning
- Social presence

The primary reaction we want from a visitor is:

> **"Simon seems like an interesting person with interesting hobbies. I want to know more."**

The site should encourage **discovery** rather than attempting to communicate everything immediately.

---

# 2. Identity Model

The website must clearly distinguish between three related but different concepts.

## Simon

**Simon is the person.**

He is the primary identity of the website and the person visitors should ultimately feel they are getting to know.

The majority of the website should therefore be presented from Simon's perspective.

## Y-Gaming

**Y-Gaming is Simon's gaming and content brand.**

It is the brand under which Simon creates:

- YouTube content
- Streaming content
- Gaming-related content
- Other creator activities

Y-Gaming is **not** the umbrella organization for everything Simon does.

The website itself belongs to Simon, while Y-Gaming represents one important part of his creator identity.

## Yash Raj Kapoor

**Yash Raj Kapoor is Simon's formal/professional identity.**

This name should primarily appear where formal context is appropriate, including:

- Professional portfolio
- Resume/CV
- Work history
- Formal professional information

The website should make these identities feel naturally connected without over-explaining the relationship.

---

# 3. Core Concept

The site should feel like:

> **Simon's personal corner of the internet.**

It should not feel like:

- A résumé
- A LinkedIn profile
- A corporate website
- A Linktree
- A Y-Gaming corporate site
- A generic developer portfolio

Professional achievements are important, but they are **part of the story rather than the entire story**.

---

# 4. What the Website Should Communicate

## Creating

Simon enjoys creating things.

This includes:

- Websites
- Discord bots
- Software
- Tools
- Artwork
- Content
- Experiments

The exact medium is less important than the underlying characteristic:

> **Simon likes turning ideas into things.**

## Learning

Simon enjoys learning and trying new things.

The website should therefore be able to showcase **what he's currently exploring**, even when it isn't directly related to his career.

For example:

> Currently exploring: 3D printing

This should be a living part of the site rather than a permanent biography item.

## Working

Simon works as a **Community Manager at BlueStacks**, specifically within the **BotLabs team**, which works on Discord bots including:

- Carl-bot
- YAGPDB

His professional experience also includes development and/or work involving Discord bots for organizations such as:

- NetEase
- Tencent
- ByteDance

Professional information should establish credibility without overwhelming the personal aspects of the site.

## Creating as a Creator

Y-Gaming represents Simon's gaming/content side.

This includes:

- YouTube
- Streaming
- Gaming content
- Creator activities

Y-Gaming should be presented as a distinct brand while remaining naturally connected to Simon.

## Enjoying

The website should show what Simon enjoys outside work.

Potential interests include:

- Gaming
- Anime
- Music / K-pop
- Movies
- Books
- Art
- Streaming
- Other hobbies

These should feel like genuine glimpses into Simon rather than a checklist of personality traits.

---

# 5. Flagship Project — ValorantRank.Chat

**ValorantRank.Chat** is one of Simon's most significant independent projects and should receive prominent treatment.

Current facts:

- Built and operated by Simon
- Free for users
- More than **11 million uses**
- Used by professional Valorant players on their streams

The project demonstrates:

- Software development
- Problem solving
- Independent initiative
- Community utility
- Long-term maintenance
- Real-world adoption

It should therefore be presented as a **featured project/case study**, rather than simply another project card.

The project can serve as an example of the broader idea:

> **Simon likes making things that people actually use.**

---

# 6. Creative Work

Simon has created artwork that he is proud of.

Artwork should have a place on the website independent from professional development work.

The website should support an evolving gallery that can accommodate:

- Existing artwork
- Future artwork
- Different styles
- Individual pieces
- Potential future creative projects

The artwork should contribute to the sense that Simon is a multidimensional creator rather than solely a technical professional.

---

# 7. Target Audience

The website should serve multiple audiences without becoming fragmented.

### Friends / people who already know Simon

They should discover aspects of Simon they didn't previously know.

### People who know Y-Gaming

They should understand the person behind the gaming/content brand.

### Professional contacts

They should quickly be able to find:

- Professional background
- Work experience
- Projects
- Resume/CV
- Contact information

### People discovering a project

Someone arriving through ValorantRank.Chat or another project should be able to naturally discover the person behind it.

---

# 10. Homepage Objective

The homepage should answer three questions without requiring significant reading:

### Who is this?

**Simon.**

### What kind of person is he?

Someone who enjoys **creating, learning and exploring things.**

### Why should I explore further?

Because there are interesting projects, hobbies and experiences waiting to be discovered.

The homepage should **not** attempt to contain Simon's complete biography.

---

# 11. Homepage Concept

The current preferred direction combines:

### Identity-first

The opening focuses on Simon as a person.

### Personal hub

The rest of the homepage provides curated windows into different aspects of his life.

Conceptually:

Hero  
↓  
Who I am  
↓  
What I do  
↓  
What I build  
↓  
What I'm exploring  
↓  
What I enjoy  
↓  
Y-Gaming  
↓

This is an information model, not a final visual layout.

---

# 12. Design Philosophy

The design should be:

- Primarily dark
- Minimal
- Clean
- Modern
- Personal
- Calm
- Slightly technical
- Creative without being chaotic

The preferred balance is:

> **Professional enough for work, personal enough to feel like Simon.**

The website should avoid becoming a stereotypical gaming website despite Simon's gaming interests.

---

# 13. Animation & Interaction

Animation should be limited and purposeful.

Appropriate examples:

- Hover effects
- Page transitions
- Subtle entrance animations
- Navigation transitions
- Image reveals
- Small interactive details

Avoid excessive animation, background effects or visual spectacle.

The site should remain fast and comfortable to use.

---

# 14. Content Philosophy

The website should be **living and expandable**.

Simon should be able to add:

- New projects
- New hobbies
- New artwork
- New social platforms
- New achievements
- New interests
- New things he's learning
- New creator activities

without needing to redesign the entire website.

The architecture should therefore separate **content from presentation** wherever practical.

---

# 15. Privacy Boundary

The website is personal but not private.

### Explicitly private:

- Relationships
- Family

These should not be represented on the public website.

Other personal interests and experiences can be considered for inclusion based on whether they contribute meaningfully to the public version of Simon.

---

# 16. Technical Direction

Initial technical direction:

- Static website
- GitHub repository
- GitHub Pages
- Custom domain: `y-gaming.in`
- React
- Vite
- TypeScript
- Responsive design
- Component-based architecture
- Structured content/data
- No backend for the initial version

The implementation should remain relatively simple and portable.

---

# 17. Maintainability

The website should be designed to be easy for both Simon and AI agents to maintain.

For example, social links, projects and interests should ideally live in structured data rather than being scattered throughout page components.

Adding:

> "I started 3D printing"

should be a content change, not an architectural change.

The same principle should apply to future projects and social platforms.

---

# 18. MVP

The first release should prove the concept without attempting to represent Simon's entire life.

### MVP:

1. Homepage
2. About
3. Professional overview
4. Projects
5. Featured ValorantRank.Chat showcase
6. Basic creator/Y-Gaming section
7. Basic interests section
8. Social/Connect section
9. Responsive design

11. Custom domain
12. SEO/social metadata

Artwork, detailed hobby pages, individual case studies and other content can follow after the core experience is established.

# 20. Success Criteria

## Personal

Visitors understand **who Simon is**, not merely what his job is.

## Professional

A professional visitor can quickly find credible information about Simon's career and work.

## Memorable

A visitor leaves thinking:

> **"Simon seems like an interesting person."**

rather than:

> "That's a nice portfolio."

## Discoverable

Visitors can naturally move between work, projects, creator activities and interests.

## Maintainable

Simon can continually update the site without rebuilding its architecture.

## Authentic

The site feels like something Simon would actually use and maintain, rather than an AI-generated portfolio template.

## Fast

The site loads quickly and remains responsive across devices.

## Expandable

The website can grow alongside Simon's projects and interests.

---

# 21. Product North Star

> **The website should show who Simon is, not simply list what Simon has done.**

His work, projects, artwork, hobbies, creator activities and current interests are the different windows through which visitors discover him.

The site doesn't need to convince people that Simon is interesting.

**It just needs to give them enough interesting things to discover.**
